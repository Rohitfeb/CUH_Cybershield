Cybersecurity learning resources

Stay Alert. Stay Secure.

Learn to recognize online threats and protect your personal, academic and campus accounts.

Strong Passwords

Use a unique, long password for every account and enable multi-factor authentication.

  • Prefer a passphrase of several unrelated words.
  • Do not reuse passwords across campus and personal accounts.
  • Use a trusted password manager.

Recognize Phishing

Unexpected urgency, unusual links and requests for credentials are common warning signs.

  • Verify the sender through a known official channel.
  • Check links before opening them.
  • Never share passwords or one-time codes.

Safe Downloads

Download files only from trusted sources and keep devices updated.

  • Avoid pirated or unofficial software.
  • Inspect unexpected attachments before opening them.
  • Use current antivirus and security updates.

Protect Your Identity

Limit personal information shared publicly and confirm privacy settings regularly.

  • Verify contacts before sharing sensitive details.
  • Be cautious about location and travel posts.
  • Lock devices when left unattended.

Social Engineering

Attackers may impersonate staff, friends or support teams to gain trust or urgency.

  • Pause before approving unusual requests.
  • Confirm instructions using a separate channel.
  • Do not bypass security procedures under pressure.

Report Suspicious Activity

Quick reporting helps administrators reduce risk for the wider campus community.

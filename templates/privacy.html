Privacy and Data Use

Information collected

CUH Cyber Shield stores account details, quiz attempts, incident reports, and security audit events needed to operate the platform.

How information is used

Information is used for authentication, cybersecurity awareness, incident response, role-based administration, and system security monitoring.

Account deletion

Authenticated students and faculty can delete their account from the dashboard. Associated quiz attempts and incident reports and uploaded evidence are removed with the account.

Security

Passwords are hashed. Sensitive reset tokens expire and become unusable after a password change. State-changing requests are protected with CSRF validation. Incident evidence is stored outside public static files and can only be downloaded by incident-management staff.
